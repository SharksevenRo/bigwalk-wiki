## Can I rebind controls in Big Walk?

Yes — **all controls can be rebound** in Settings > Rebinding. Several controls also offer a choice between **Hold** and **Toggle** behavior, and you can pick from **multiple crosshair styles**. Both mouse & keyboard and controllers work well: the computer version supports most game controllers, and the game even supports the Nintendo Switch 2's mouse mode.

![A hiker adjusts their gear at the trailhead before a long walk](/images/articles/bw-settings.jpg)

## What helps with motion sickness?

Two settings, per the official FAQ:

- **Motion Reduction Overlays** (Settings > General) — unique overlays designed to keep you feeling grounded in a first-person world.
- **Field of View** (Settings > Graphics, PC and Mac only) — a higher number shows more of the world and can feel less claustrophobic.

If you're on PC and still stuttering, try lowering Render Scale, then Quality, then raising Render Scale back gradually — the FAQ recommends this order to balance CPU against GPU load.

## Can I play without voice chat?

Yes. **Text chat works anywhere voice chat does, with the same restrictions** — your text isn't readable when you're far away or separated by soundproof glass, and it works through in-game items like walkie talkies. So a text-only group loses nothing mechanical. No standalone microphone? DualSense controllers, Switch 2, Steam Deck, and most laptops have built-ins. Details live in the [voice chat guide](/guides/voice-chat/).

## What about skipping hard challenges?

Any challenge can be **skipped** outright — an explicit accessibility feature, not a cheat. If one mechanism is blocking your group's evening, skip it and keep walking.

## Controller troubleshooting (Mac + Steam)

If an Xbox wireless controller misbehaves on Mac through Steam, the FAQ's fix list:

1. Connect via **Bluetooth**, not USB-C direct.
2. In System Settings > Game Controllers > Xbox Wireless Controller, create a profile for Steam and toggle on **Increase compatibility**.
3. Grant Steam both permissions: **Privacy & Security > Accessibility** and **Privacy & Security > Input Monitoring**.
4. Enable **Steam Input** with the default Big Walk controller profile.

## Platform-specific voice chat caveats

- **PS Portal cloud streaming:** in-game voice chat is currently broken ("Communication disabled while party chat is active"). Voice works as expected when streaming from your PS5 via Remote Play.
- **macOS:** enable the microphone for Big Walk under Privacy & Security, or the game hears nothing.
- Voice chat volume got a dedicated fix in [patch 1.4.8](/guides/update-history/) after launch — if voices still feel quiet, confirm everyone's actually on it.
