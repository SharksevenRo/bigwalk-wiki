## How many players does Big Walk support?

Big Walk is built for groups of **2 to 12 players**. There is no solo mode — the island's puzzles assume at least one partner — and there is no public matchmaking. You always walk with people you deliberately invite, which is why the game's first real decision happens before the hike even starts: who hosts.

![A group of hikers waves across a grassy ridge in Big Walk](/images/articles/bw-multiplayer.jpg)

<Callout type="tip">Puzzles are designed around groups of up to four. Bigger groups work, but splitting into squads of three or four keeps everyone on a mechanism instead of waiting for a turn.</Callout>

## How does hosting work?

The host is the keeper of the save game. The official FAQ spells out what that means in practice:

- The **host's progress is saved automatically** — nobody else needs to manage saves.
- The host **starts the session first**; only then can others connect.
- **When the host stops the session, all other players are disconnected.** To continue later, the same host must host again.

Treat the host as your group's archivist: pick the most reliable player, and agree on a schedule before anyone builds five hours of progress.

## How do players join a session?

Two doors in, per the official FAQ:

1. **Platform friends list.** If you're friends on Steam, PlayStation, or your console platform, the host's active game appears in a menu of friend sessions automatically.
2. **Join Code.** A short, unique code generated every time the host starts a session, shared however you like. Note that the **Mac App Store version does not support Game Center, so Mac App Store players must use a Join Code**.

The host can also change the session password mid-game from the in-game menu under **Session Details**.

## Is Big Walk cross-platform?

Yes. PC (Steam and Mac) and consoles play together — a Steam player can join a PS5-hosted session and vice versa. The console requirements are the usual ones: **PS5 players need PlayStation Plus, and Switch 2 players need Nintendo Switch Online**. Version numbers only need to agree on the **first two digits** (for example 1.4.x), so a 1.4.7 player can still join a 1.4.8 host.

## What does the world-size choice mean?

When the host starts a run, they pick a world configuration of **2, 3, or 4+ towers**. This sets the puzzle scale of the island, not a player cap — it's about how much island your group wants to chew through, not how many of you there are.

## What happens to lost items?

If something important goes missing, check the **Lost & Found at the gym** — it recovers lost items for your group.
