## Voice Is a Game Mechanic, Not a Feature

In Big Walk, your microphone is an in-world object. Other players hear you exactly as if you were standing on the island: close voices are clear, distant ones fade, walls muffle, and caverns echo. The game was designed so that **communication difficulty equals physical distance** — that's what makes splitting up meaningful. If your group plays in a Discord call instead, you're bypassing the core puzzle system.

Patch **1.4.8** (October 2026) fixed the major reliability complaints: inconsistent distance falloff, exaggerated echo in corridors, and radio static behaving erratically. If you last played before that patch and gave up on in-game voice, it's worth another try.

## The Rules of Proximity Audio

- **Distance falloff.** The farther a teammate is, the quieter they get, until you can't hear them at all. Very long distances require the radio from the [tools list](/items/getting-started/).
- **Corridor and cave echo.** Hard surfaces reflect sound — long tunnels smear speech. Slow down and enunciate underground.
- **Walls muffle.** Someone on the other side of a building sounds underwater. Move to a doorway or window line.
- **The radio has its own texture.** Walkie-talkie audio carries deliberate static and interference — readable, but you must confirm messages ("copy that").

## Text Chat Follows the Same Physics

Text chat is not a global channel. Messages inherit the same spatial rules as voice, which surprises new players: a message typed from across the island may effectively not reach your teammates. Never use text as a fallback for critical callouts — walk closer, or use the radio.

## Practical Group Protocol

1. **Test audio at spawn.** Thirty seconds of "can you hear me at this distance" prevents an hour of confusion.
2. **Assign a caller for split puzzles.** One voice per information source, as covered in [puzzle solutions](/guides/puzzle-solutions/).
3. **Adopt flashlight morse early** — one flash = yes, two = no. It becomes mandatory in the [purple challenges](/guides/purple-challenges/).
4. **Don't all talk at once.** Proximity audio doesn't mix kindly; the loudest speaker wins.

## Troubleshooting

- **Teammate too quiet?** They're probably just far away — that's the mechanic. Check the [island map](/maps/getting-started/) for how far you've drifted.
- **Weird echo indoors after 1.4.8?** Echo is intentional, but if speech becomes unintelligible, have one player step into an open area.
- **Radio unreadable?** Stand still; the static increases while moving.
