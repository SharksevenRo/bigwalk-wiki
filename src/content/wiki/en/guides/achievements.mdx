## Overview

Big Walk by House House (the studio behind Untitled Goose Game) ships with **12 achievements**, plus a Platinum trophy on PlayStation for earning all of them. Ten unlock through natural progression or simple equipment use; the other two — Big Climb and Big Makeover — require dedicated detours. Nothing is missable: the island stays open after both [endings](/guides/endings/), and all progress lives in the host's save. This list was cross-checked against multiple community sources in October 2026.

## The Trigger Rule That Explains Most "Bugged" Achievements

Nearly every stuck achievement in Big Walk comes from doing 90% of the action:

- **Opening the drawbridge ≠ crossing it.** Big Walk needs your character on the far side.
- **Picking up the Backpack ≠ wearing it.** Big Pack needs it visibly attached to your back (see [tools and toys](/items/getting-started/) for how items equip).
- **Finding the map ≠ Big View.** The trigger is entering the physical Map Room; the portable map is just an item.
- **The hip belt alone ≠ Big Help.** Attach a compatible item — the folded portable map is the cleanest test.
- **Reaching a station ≠ riding.** Big Sit and Big Ride only fire while you're physically on the moving chairlift or train.
- **Standing at a threshold ≠ entering.** Big Tunnel fires inside the tunnel, not at its door.

If an achievement doesn't pop, complete the final verb — cross, wear, enter, ride — before assuming it's broken.

## Recommended Order

1. **Big Walk** — comes naturally in the tutorial.
2. **Big Pack** — equip the first Backpack you find; give it to whoever carries progression items.
3. **Big View** — Red route progression opens the Map Room.
4. **Big Help** — fold the portable map, equip the hip belt, slot the map in.
5. **Big Sit** — Green route; board a circulating seat.
6. **Big Climb** — use the upper chairlift route, then keep climbing past it; the trigger is elevation, not the ride.
7. **Big Tunnel** — Yellow route; walk fully inside.
8. **Big Ride** — Blue route; board the moving train.
9. **Big Wall** — Black route; pass through to the far side.
10. **Big Goodbye** — complete the [normal ending](/guides/endings/).
11. **Big Game** — continue the *same save*: finish the [purple challenges](/guides/purple-challenges/) and the true ending route.
12. **Big Makeover** — optional underground Shiny route; Yellow progression opens the way in.

Chasing achievements out of order wastes time — most are checkpoints on routes you'll travel anyway.

## Big Goodbye vs Big Game

These are **not** the same unlock. Big Goodbye fires at the normal ending's credits; Big Game requires full completion afterward — the seven purple challenges, remaining unfinished puzzles, and the hidden final-key route that leads to the true ending. If Big Goodbye popped but Big Game didn't, that's expected: keep playing the same save, don't restart. See the [endings guide](/guides/endings/) for the exact split.

## Does Big Game Require Big Makeover?

Community testing says no — Big Makeover (the Shiny route) stands apart from the Big Game completion chain. Still worth grabbing while you're in the underground area, since it's the rarest achievement by far.
