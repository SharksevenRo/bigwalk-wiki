## Overview

Big Walk ships with **12 achievements** and no missable-trophy anxiety — the island stays open after both [endings](/guides/endings/), so everything can be cleaned up in the host's world. This page lists what's known so far and marks anything still being community-verified.

## Ending Achievements

| Achievement | How to unlock |
|---|---|
| **Big Walk** | Reach the credits via the [normal ending](/guides/endings/) — towers, Black Obelisk, Black Key, Black Sphere trek. |
| **Big Goodbye** | Also tied to the normal ending path's finale — earned as your group enters the silent final zone together. |

## Purple Challenge Achievements

Each of the seven [purple challenges](/guides/purple-challenges/) awards progress toward the secret-path achievements; completing the full set also contributes here. The challenges themselves — Wait 30 Minutes House, no-throw Golf, 36 Drawings, Nine Speakers, Nine Symbols, speed Platforming, and Many Lights — are the achievement backbone of the endgame.

## Tool and Co-op Achievements

A handful reward using the island's [tools and toys](/items/getting-started/) and playing like a real team — coordinating by radio, signaling with the flashlight, and navigating from the paper map rather than wandering. Exact unlock conditions for several of these are still being pinned down by the community; we'll update this table as they're confirmed in verified runs.

## Completion Tips

- **The host earns everything.** Remember progress lives in the host's world — farm achievements in the same save.
- **Sequence matters little**, but doing the normal ending before the purple challenges reduces frustration.
- Keep the group talking: many achievements trip naturally when everyone uses [voice chat properly](/guides/voice-chat/) instead of playing silently in a call.
