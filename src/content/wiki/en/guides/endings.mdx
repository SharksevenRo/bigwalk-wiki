## Spoiler Warning

This page describes both endings' full structure. If you'd rather discover the island yourself, stop here and read the [beginner's guide](/guides/getting-started/) instead. Structure only — we avoid narrative details where the impact matters.

## The Normal Ending (Big Walk / Big Goodbye)

The standard path most groups finish in one or two long sessions:

![Hikers approach the Black Tower at dusk, the island's central structure](/images/articles/bw-endings.jpg)

1. **Activate all towers.** The number depends on the host's chosen layout — 2, 3, or 4+ (see the [island map](/maps/getting-started/)).
2. **Travel to the Black Tower**, the island's central structure, once the towers are done.
3. **Claim the Black Key** along the tower route.
4. **Open the Wall** — the great barrier on the island's edge — with the Black Key.
5. **Enter the huge black sphere** beyond the Wall. Inside is a cooperative puzzle room: work through its hanging mechanisms together, then press the final button beside them.
6. **Cross the water to the small hut** on the far side. Wave goodbye to each other with the Wave Goodbye emote (Q/E on PC), press the button, and step through the door together — this is the Big Goodbye.

This path awards the **Big Walk** and **Big Goodbye** achievements, two of the game's 12 total covered on the [achievements list](/guides/achievements/).

## The True Ending (Secret)

The hidden path continues in the same save after the normal ending:

1. **Finish the normal ending first.** The true-ending content only unlocks afterward.
2. **Complete all seven [purple challenges](/guides/purple-challenges/).** They appear on the island map once the normal ending is done — no hunting for a secret door.
3. **Mop up everything remaining.** The game checks that your group has completed every unfinished puzzle and delivery on the island, not just the purple set.
4. **Find the final key.** Once the completion check passes, it appears near the spawn area where your run began.
5. **Open the hidden room** the final key unlocks, settle in for the long reading inside, and step out through the white exit — the Big Game.

The true ending reframes the normal ending rather than replacing it, which is why most groups do the normal path first.

## Which Should You Do First?

Normal, almost always. It teaches you the island's [puzzle families](/guides/puzzle-solutions/), gives you the full tool kit, and the true ending assumes that fluency — the purple challenges are endgame-difficulty. Also note: both endings live in the host's world, so agree on the plan before anyone burns five hours.

## Post-Game

After either ending, the island stays open. Twelve [achievements](/guides/achievements/) mean there's cleanup to do, and patch 1.4.8 (August 2026) fixed overly quiet audio for exactly these long co-op sessions.
