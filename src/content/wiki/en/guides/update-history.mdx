## When did Big Walk come out?

Big Walk version 1.0 released on **August 4, 2026**, across Steam, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2 (SteamDB). The Steam launch shipped with a **25% launch discount** and a **Big Walk + PEAK bundle** for players who wanted House House's earlier co-op hiking game in the same purchase. On PlayStation, Big Walk was also a **PS Plus Essential monthly game for August**, with a claim window running to roughly September 1.

![Hikers gather at the island's edge beneath a patch-note sunset](/images/articles/bw-updates.jpg)

## What did patch 1.4.8 change?

The launch's loudest complaint was audio: nearby teammates' voices were too quiet, and House House acknowledged the problem publicly on **August 5**. Patch **1.4.8** followed on August 6–7 (Steam first, with console versions trailing), and per the patch notes it addressed the too-quiet voice issue.

<Callout type="warn">The fix raised the volume of nearby voice — it did not extend voice pickup distance. If a teammate is genuinely far away, you still can't hear them, by design. Plan your [group communication](/guides/voice-chat/) around that constraint.</Callout>

## Known issues still open

House House has acknowledged, but not yet fixed, as of patch 1.4.8:

- **Audio desync in parties of 10 or more players** — big-group hikes can drift out of sync.
- **Matchmaking timeouts at peak hours** — sessions can time out when connecting during busy windows.

## How do I keep the game updated?

Version numbers only need to match on the **first two digits** for cross-play, so a 1.4.7 player can still join a 1.4.8 host — handy when console patches trail Steam. Console players should let platform auto-updates run; Steam players can verify integrity if a friend can't see their session. For what the host needs to do before anyone updates mid-run, see the [multiplayer hosting guide](/guides/multiplayer-hosting/) — and if you're new to the island altogether, start with the [beginner's guide](/guides/getting-started/).
