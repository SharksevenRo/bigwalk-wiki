## Why Tools Matter More Than Skills

Big Walk has no character stats, no levels, and no inventory builds. Every ability your group has comes from physical items scattered around the island. Most puzzles assume people split up and combine tools: one player marks a target with the laser pointer while a teammate across a valley confirms it by radio. The [beginner's guide](/guides/getting-started/) covers session basics; this page is the item catalog.

![Flat lay of the island tools: radio, flashlight, laser pointer, binoculars, megaphone, whiteboard, backpack](/images/articles/bw-tools.jpg)

## Every Tool in the Game

### Radio (Walkie-Talkie)

The single most important item. Lets you talk to players who are too far away for proximity voice — with deliberate static and interference so it still feels diegetic. When a puzzle splits the group across the map, the radio is the mechanic.

### Flashlight

A light source, but its real use is signaling. Several late-game challenges revolve around flashing light patterns: one flash means "yes," two flashes means "no." See [purple challenges](/guides/purple-challenges/) for where this gets demanding.

### Laser Pointer

Marks distant objects so other players can see the dot. Essential for directing teammates' attention across long sightlines — "aim at the symbol you mean" beats describing it over voice chat.

### Binoculars

Long-range spotting tool. Combines with the laser pointer: one player scans a cliff face with binoculars and calls out targets, another marks them.

### Megaphone

Projects your voice over distance with a directional cone. Useful for one-way announcements to a whole area — and for the challenge that forbids normal talking entirely.

### Whiteboard

A handheld board you can draw on. The classic use is sketching a symbol you can't describe in words so a teammate can reproduce it elsewhere — central to the drawing-based puzzle family.

### Backpack

Lets one player carry multiple items at once, turning them into the group's designated "mule." Handy when a puzzle requires four tools but only two people made the trip.

## Which Puzzles Need Which Tools

- **Symbol-copying puzzles** → whiteboard + radio or binoculars
- **Long-distance coordination** → laser pointer + radio
- **Darkness or signaling** → flashlight
- **One-way instructions** → megaphone
- **Multi-item fetch quests** → backpack

The full puzzle families are cataloged in the [puzzle solutions walkthrough](/guides/puzzle-solutions/), and the island's landmarks — where many tools are found — are on the [island map](/maps/getting-started/).

## Notes on Patch 1.4.8

The October 2026 patch focused on voice chat reliability (proximity falloff, echo in corridors, radio static behavior). No item functions changed in 1.4.8. If tool behavior ever changes in a future patch, we'll update this page with the exact version.

## Community Tips

- Agree on a group "radio protocol" early: who calls, how you confirm ("copy that").
- Don't let one person hoard every tool — the backpack mule should hand items off at puzzle sites.
- If the flashlight signaling feels unreliable, stand still; moving targets are hard to count flashes on.
