## Where are the major landmarks on the island?

The community-maintained landmark directory on bigwalk.art lists the island's major points of interest with in-game coordinates. Use these as anchors when your group splits up:

| Landmark | Approximate coordinates |
| --- | --- |
| Red Tower | 3670, 1400 |
| Map Room | 3560, 1425 |
| Blue Tower | 3540, 1850 |
| Yellow Tower | 3310, 1560 |
| Yellow Tunnel | 3500, 1620 |
| Train station | 3380, 1740 |
| Cable car | 3950, 1950 |

Beyond the towers, the directory also tracks **seven radio points**, the **Lost & Found** (where lost items turn up), and the **Customisation Room**. The Red Tower is the island's central structure — and the gateway to the map itself.

## How do I unlock the full island map?

The island hides a proper cartography station. According to Polygon's map guide, the unlock chain runs like this:

1. **Collect five gourd-shaped collectibles** — they hang around the island like oversized calabashes.
2. **Trade them at the Red Tower** for a key.
3. **Shave the key down**, following the arrows, until it fits.
4. **Find the blue door with red stairs** and use the shaved key on it.
5. Inside is the **Map Room** — with the full island map on the wall.

<Callout type="info">The Map Room stocks three laser pointers for pointing at the wall map, a compass, and a small paper map you can carry. The big wall map shows the island but does not mark challenge locations — those you discover by walking.</Callout>

## Why bother with the Map Room?

Two reasons. First, a shared wall map changes how a group navigates: one player can point the laser at a tower while everyone else argues about the route, which beats describing landmarks over [voice chat](/guides/voice-chat/) from opposite sides of the island. Second, the carryable paper map means your group's navigator can scout ahead without dragging everyone along.

If you're still learning the island's layout, the [island map overview](/maps/getting-started/) covers the basics, and the [puzzle solutions guide](/guides/puzzle-solutions/) explains what to do once you reach each landmark.
